# 90-day growth roadmap: MJG Cleaning Services

**Goal:** Make it easy for people in Timmins to find MJG, trust MJG and ask for a quote.

This plan is based on the [website audit](01-website-audit.md).

## The short version

Your cleaning work may be excellent, but a stranger looking at your website can't tell yet. The next 90 days fix that in three stages:

| Stage | Days | Focus | By the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stop losing leads** | 1–30 | Quick website fixes, your Google listing, reviews and photos | People can call you in one tap, and proof starts building |
| **Launch the new website** | 31–60 | New site, quote form, gallery and service pages | You have a site that looks as good as your work |
| **Grow what works** | 61–90 | Online booking for repeat customers, commercial outreach, follow-ups | You know where your leads come from and can do more of it |

**If you start in November:** Days 1–90 run through the holidays and into winter. Promote pre-holiday deep cleans and New Year office resets, and have your spring cleaning offer ready for March.

## What we need from you

Your input is what makes this work. Plan on about **2–3 hours a week**, plus a few minutes at each job for photos and review requests.

**Five decisions to make in week 1:**

1. What's the one public business name you want to use everywhere?
2. Which towns and communities will you really travel to?
3. What guarantee are you comfortable putting in writing?
4. Do you have insurance and WSIB coverage we can mention?
5. Who answers new leads, and how fast can they reply during business hours?

## Days 1–30: Stop losing leads

### Lead generation

- [ ] Put your phone number at the top of the homepage, tappable on a phone.
- [ ] Add a phone number box to your contact form.
- [ ] Fix the three "More info" buttons, which currently go nowhere.
- [ ] Fill in your blank About Us page.
- [ ] Start a simple lead list with date, name, service, where they heard about you, and whether they booked.

### Customer trust

- [ ] Write a short owner story: who you are, when you started, and why you care.
- [ ] Gather your insurance and WSIB documents.
- [ ] Write your guarantee in one or two plain sentences.
- [ ] Take a friendly photo of yourself and your team.

### SEO (being found on Google)

- [ ] Claim or create your free Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Complete every part of it: services, hours, service area, photos and description.
- [ ] Update your website's Google title and description to mention cleaning and Timmins.
- [ ] Check that your name and phone number match everywhere they appear online.

### Reviews

- [ ] Set up a short link that goes straight to your Google review page.
- [ ] Message your past customers to ask for an honest review.
- [ ] Ask every new customer after every job.

> **A message you can copy:**
> "Hi [name], thanks for choosing MJG Cleaning. If you have a minute, would you share an honest review of your experience? It really helps a small local business: [link]. If anything wasn't right, please tell us so we can fix it."

### Before and after photos

- [ ] Start taking photos at every suitable job, using the checklist below.
- [ ] Aim for **4–6 photo sets** this month.

### You'll know this stage worked when

- Your phone number is tappable and every form asks for a callback number.
- Your Google listing is live and complete.
- Your first reviews are coming in.
- Your lead list is being used.

## Days 31–60: Launch the new website

### Lead generation

- [ ] Approve the homepage wording and design.
- [ ] Launch the new website with a separate page for each service.
- [ ] Add Call and Free Quote buttons that stay on screen on phones.
- [ ] Have every new quote request sent to your phone right away.
- [ ] Turn on tracking for calls and quote requests.

### Online booking, step 1: a simple quote request

Start with a short form that asks what needs cleaning, the community and the preferred days. You confirm the price and time yourself.

Cleaning jobs vary a lot, so online instant pricing could lock you into prices that don't fit the job. Add it only once you have a price list you trust.

### Customer trust

- [ ] Publish your story, photo, guarantee and insurance details.
- [ ] Show your best real reviews, with the customers' permission.

### SEO

- [ ] Publish your residential, commercial, deep cleaning and service area pages.
- [ ] Connect the new site to Google's free website tools so it can be found quickly.
- [ ] Make sure old website addresses forward to the new pages.
- [ ] Post new photos to your Google listing every week.

### Reviews

- [ ] Reply to every review, good or bad, within a couple of days.
- [ ] Keep asking every customer.

### Before and after gallery

- [ ] Launch the gallery with your **best 8–10 photo sets**, sorted by service.
- [ ] Add a line to each: what the job was and the community it was in.

### You'll know this stage worked when

- The new site is live and works well on your phone.
- You receive a text within moments of a test quote request.
- The gallery contains only real MJG jobs.

## Days 61–90: Grow what works

### Lead generation

- [ ] Review your lead list. Where did leads come from, and which ones booked?
- [ ] Follow up on quotes after 2 days, then again after 7 days.
- [ ] Ask each one-time customer whether they'd like a regular schedule.
- [ ] Consider a small test of Google Ads or local Facebook ads, only once your site and reviews are ready.

### Commercial clients

- [ ] Create a one-page commercial summary with your services, insurance, WSIB, references and contact details.
- [ ] Contact **10–20 nearby businesses** each month that fit your capacity, such as offices, clinics, shops and property managers.
- [ ] Offer each one a free walkthrough and written quote.

### Online booking, step 2: easy rebooking

- [ ] Let repeat customers request their next clean online.
- [ ] Send appointment reminders by text or email.
- [ ] Add a live calendar only if your schedule stays predictable enough to keep it accurate.

### SEO

- [ ] Publish one helpful article or job story every two weeks, such as "Getting road salt out of your entryway".
- [ ] List your business on Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, BBB and HomeStars.
- [ ] See which Google searches are bringing people in, and add to those pages.

### Reviews and gallery

- [ ] Add new reviews to your website each month.
- [ ] Share one before-and-after set a week on Facebook and your Google listing.
- [ ] Reach **12 or more** gallery sets, including commercial jobs when clients agree.

### You'll know this stage worked when

- You can name your best source of leads.
- Quotes no longer go without a follow-up.
- You have a repeatable monthly routine for reviews, photos and outreach.

## Your weekly scoreboard

Spend 10 minutes on this each week. Record your current numbers in week 1 so you can see the change.

| Measure | Why it matters | Suggested 90-day target |
|---|---|---|
| Phone calls from the website and Google | Your hottest leads | Going up month over month |
| Quote requests | Shows whether the site is working | Going up month over month |
| Quotes that become booked jobs | Shows whether leads and follow-up are good | Set after 30 days of tracking |
| Time to reply to a new lead | Fast replies are less likely to lose out to a competitor | Under 1 hour during business hours |
| New Google reviews | Builds trust and helps your Google listing | 15–25 total, if job volume allows |
| Before-and-after sets | Shows real proof | 12 or more |
| Repeat customers | Steady, predictable income | Set after 30 days of tracking |

These are working targets, not promises. Your starting numbers aren't known yet, so adjust them after the first month.

## Before-and-after photo checklist

- [ ] **Get permission first**, ideally in writing, from the customer or building owner.
- [ ] Stand in the same spot for both photos, at the same angle.
- [ ] Turn the lights on, and hold the phone sideways.
- [ ] Focus on one clear problem area, such as a stove, shower or entry floor.
- [ ] Leave out faces, family photos, mail, street numbers, computer screens and anything confidential.
- [ ] Be honest: no filters, no borrowed photos, and no staged "before" shots.

## Rules to protect your reputation

**Google reviews**

- ✅ Ask for honest reviews.
- ❌ Don't offer discounts, gifts or free cleans in exchange for reviews.
- ❌ Don't ask only your happy customers. Google doesn't allow selectively asking for positive reviews.
- ❌ Don't discourage negative reviews or tell people what to write.
- ❌ Don't give staff a quota of reviews to collect. The targets above are a business goal only.
- ❌ Don't post reviews yourself, or ask friends who've never been customers.

Reviews that break these rules can be removed. See [Google's policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114).

**On your website**

- Publish only real reviews and real job photos. Canada's Competition Act also prohibits misleading advertising, so reviews and claims must be genuine. This isn't legal advice.
- Only claim insurance, WSIB coverage or guarantees that are accurate, and keep them up to date.

## What could get in the way

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| You're too busy cleaning to do this | Build photos and review requests into each job. Block one fixed hour a week for the rest. |
| Customers don't want photos taken | Photograph only a small area, such as a stove or floor, with nothing personal showing. |
| A bad review appears | Reply calmly, offer to fix it, and move the conversation offline. Never argue in public. |
| More leads than you can handle | Reply anyway, offer the next available date, and raise commercial outreach only when you have capacity. |
| The new website takes longer than planned | Your quick fixes, Google listing, reviews and photos don't depend on it. Keep going. |
